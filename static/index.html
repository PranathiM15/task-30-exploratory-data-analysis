Know the fundamentals.

What is EDA?

Exploratory Data Analysis is the process of summarizing and visualizing data to understand its structure, quality and patterns.

Why before machine learning?

It helps identify missing values, unusual records, class imbalance and relationships before training a model.

Common EDA steps?

Inspect shape and types, check missing values and duplicates, calculate statistics, visualize distributions and document findings.
