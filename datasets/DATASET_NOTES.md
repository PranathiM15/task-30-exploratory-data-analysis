# Dataset notes

- `iris.csv`: 150 genuine classic Iris records, 4 measurements and species label.
- `titanic_sample_synthetic.csv`: 891 synthetic rows and 15 Titanic-style fields. These are generated sample values for demonstrating EDA; they are not authentic passenger records.
- `student_performance_sample_synthetic.csv`: 395 synthetic rows and 33 student-performance-style fields. The columns follow the structure described for the UCI Student Performance dataset, but the rows are generated teaching examples and are not authentic UCI records.

The Titanic and Student Performance CSV names intentionally include `sample_synthetic` to keep their status visible in file explorers and GitHub.
